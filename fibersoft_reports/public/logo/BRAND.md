# Fibersoft Reports logo kit

The fork uses a new F monogram and the Fibersoft Reports wordmark. The SVG masters and PNG exports cover light, dark, monochrome, mini, bilingual and app-icon variants. Blue: #1463FF; ink: #0C1322. Wordmarks use Arial or a compatible sans-serif fallback. PNGs are rendered from the SVG masters.
